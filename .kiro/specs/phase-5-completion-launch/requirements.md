# Requirements Document (outline)

## Introduction

Phase 5 completes v1 and launches it. Detailed acceptance criteria and tasks are written when the
phase starts.

Sources: `docs/product-decisions.md` (Personal data; Reports and exports; One product for every
size), `docs/rebuild-plan.md` (sections 4 and 6).

## Requirements (to be expanded)

1. **Role dashboards:** employee, manager and finance/admin dashboards with fixed cards.
2. **GDPR:** export everything about a person; erase with anonymised history; retention rules per
   workspace applied by schedule.
3. **Load and performance:** 100k-member workspace meets the targets for lists, search, reach
   checks and capacity totals.
4. **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 AA audit of every screen at three widths; issues fixed.
5. **Security:** review of every policy and RPC; dependency audit; preparation for a penetration
   test.
6. **Operations:** backups and point-in-time recovery on production, monitoring and alerts,
   runbook for incidents.
7. **Legal:** data processing agreement, sub-processor list, privacy policy, terms.
8. **Documentation:** user help for each module, admin guide for workspace setup and import.
9. **Launch:** production deploy, demo workspaces removed, launch checklist complete.
